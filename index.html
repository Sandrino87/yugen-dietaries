How to read the colours and the diets
  • ✓ Stated: the Food Notes say it explicitly.
  • ✓ Confirm: no conflicting ingredient in the notes, but they don't declare it. Fine for a preference; for an allergy, ask.
  • ↺ Adapt: the notes say the kitchen can remove or swap it. Write it on the docket.
  • ? Ask: the notes are unclear or contradict themselves. These questions are already on the Chef list.
  • ✕ No: it contains the ingredient and the notes don't say it can come off. You can still add “can it be done without?” to the Chef list.

Diets in one line. Pescatarian: no meat, fish ok. Buddhist vegetarian: no meat and no onion/garlic. Jain: no meat, egg, onion/garlic, honey; strict Jains also avoid root vegetables. Kosher-style: no pork, shellfish or meat with dairy; the kitchen is not certified. Coeliac: a medical condition, even a crumb counts, so always ask about shared fryers.

With several needs at once, a dish takes its worst verdict, because one wrong ingredient is enough to make it unservable.
